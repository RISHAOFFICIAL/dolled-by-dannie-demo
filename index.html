Meet Dannie

Your Detroit area braider for knotless, stitch, feed-in and goddess styles. 18K strong on Instagram and growing, one flawless install at a time.

Every style is done with care, clean parts, and attention to detail, so you leave feeling dolled and confident.

@dolledbydannie on Instagram

Hair Included

Most styles include the hair, so you do not have to shop for braiding hair before your appointment.

Human hair upgrades are available on select goddess styles. Check the service list for human hair options and pricing.

Questions about a style? Text or call (832) 580-0756.

Good to Know

  • Please come washed and FULLY blow dried from root to tip. Hair must be blown out completely for your style.
  • A deposit is required to secure your appointment. See the booking page for details.
  • Please arrive on time. Late arrivals may need to reschedule.
  • Located in Southfield / Detroit, MI. Exact address shared after booking.
