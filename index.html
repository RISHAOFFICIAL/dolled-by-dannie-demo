Meet Dannielle

Dannielle is a Detroit and Southfield braider known for one thing above all else: stitch braids that stay neat for weeks. Clean parts, smooth feedins, and a flat install that grows out gracefully instead of frizzing up by day three.

Her chair is built on consistency. Every stitch braid service includes the hair, every appointment starts on time, and every client leaves with a style that looks just as good in week three as it did on day one.

With 18K followers on Instagram, her work speaks for itself. Follow @dolledbydannie to see the latest styles, openings, and client transformations.
